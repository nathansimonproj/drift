Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Drift is a sleep-forecast tool: you log things that affect your sleep (caffeine, alcohol, nicotine, marijuana) and Drift estimates how they'll affect you tonight. This page explains, in plain language, what information Drift collects, why, and what it does — and doesn't — do with it.

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address and password you sign up with. Your password is never stored — only a one-way bcrypt hash of it.
  • Profile info (optional): name, sex, height, weight, and your target bedtime, if you choose to fill them in under Profile.
  • Sleep-affecting event logs: what you log (type, amount, and time) — caffeine, alcohol, nicotine, or marijuana entries. This is the data your forecast score is calculated from.

What we don't collect

Drift has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, and doesn't use device location, contacts, camera, or HealthKit. We don't know how you use the app beyond the data you explicitly log.

How your data is used

Solely to run Drift for you: computing your sleep forecast and score, and showing your own history back to you. Nothing more.

We do not sell, share, rent, or report your data to anyone — not to advertisers, employers, insurers, researchers, or any other third party. Your logs are visible only to you.

Third parties

Drift uses SendGrid to send password-reset emails. SendGrid only ever sees the email address needed to deliver that one email — never your profile or event data. That's the only third-party service Drift uses.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account → Delete account in the app. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated event history — this cannot be undone and we cannot recover it afterward.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, never stored in plain text. The iOS app stores your session token in the device Keychain (not in a plain preferences file). All network traffic runs over HTTPS.

Children's privacy

Drift is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
